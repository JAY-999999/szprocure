FAQ

How fast will I get a quotation?

Our sourcing team typically responds within 1 business day. Single part-number checks via WhatsApp are often answered the same day.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

No. We support everything from prototype quantities to volume production runs.

How do you ensure parts are genuine?

We source through verified channels and can provide traceability and original manufacturer documentation on request.

What files can I attach?

BOM, Excel, CSV, PDF, specifications and datasheets. Larger files can be shared via a link.
