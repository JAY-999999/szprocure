About SZ Procure

Your Shenzhen Partner For Electronics Sourcing

We connect global buyers with China's electronics supply chain — from single hard-to-find parts to full BOM sourcing.

Who we are

SZ Procure is a Shenzhen-based electronic components sourcing service. We help overseas engineers, procurement teams and manufacturers access China's electronics supply chain — whether you need a single scarce IC or a complete bill of materials.

Our work is practical: confirm real availability and lead time, compare sourcing options, and coordinate verification before shipment. No marketplace markup, no minimum-order theater — just a direct line to Shenzhen's supplier network.

Many global buyers face challenges sourcing components from China, including supplier verification, availability checking and delivery coordination. SZ Procure helps connect buyers with suitable suppliers and sourcing solutions.

What we do

Sourcing services built around how overseas buyers actually buy from China.

Component sourcing

Send a part number, specification or quantity and we match it against China's electronics channels — confirming real stock and lead time.

BOM procurement

AI hardware sourcing

Hard-to-find components

How we work

Every request starts with what you actually need: a part number, a BOM, or a specification. We check Shenzhen availability, compare options on price and lead time, and confirm before you commit.

You get a human reply — not an automated quote engine. Most requests receive a first reply within one business day (Shenzhen time, GMT+8); same-day replies are common for single part-number checks via WhatsApp.

What to include

  • Exact part number (and manufacturer if known)
  • Quantity and target price (if any)
  • Required package / grade
  • Delivery destination & deadline
