WHO WE ARE

Who We Are

SZ Procure is a professional sourcing partner helping global buyers access reliable electronic component supply solutions from China.

As a sourcing partner built for global buyers, we connect your requirements with suitable supply resources through professional communication, supplier evaluation, and hands-on sourcing support — acting as an extension of your procurement team rather than a one-off vendor.

We understand what global buyers truly care about is not “who is cheapest”, but “who can reliably, transparently, and consistently help me get the right parts.” That is exactly the position we have held from day one — a sourcing partner worth trusting.

WHY WE EXIST

Why We Exist

Building a steadier bridge between global buyers and China's electronics supply resources.

The global electronics industry runs on efficient supply chains and dependable sourcing connections. Yet for many buyers, reaching the right supply resources still means scattered information, unclear risks, and slow responses.

SZ Procure was created to bridge that gap — to help buyers simplify sourcing and build stronger, more confident connections with trusted supply resources.
