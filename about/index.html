About SZ Procure

Shenzhen-based electronic components sourcing

We connect global buyers with China's electronics supply chain — from single hard-to-find parts to full BOM sourcing.

Who we are

SZ Procure is a Shenzhen-based electronic components sourcing company. We help overseas engineers, procurement teams and manufacturers access China's electronics supply chain — whether you need a single scarce IC or a complete bill of materials.

Our work is practical: confirm real availability and lead time, compare sourcing options, and coordinate verification before shipment. No marketplace markup, no minimum-order theater — just a direct line to Shenzhen's supplier network.

What we do best

Shenzhen location

Based in Shenzhen — at the center of China's electronics manufacturing and distribution cluster. Local presence means faster supplier communication and on-the-ground verification.

Supplier network

Relationships across Shenzhen distributors, franchised and independent channels — used to confirm availability, grade and lead time rather than guess.

BOM sourcing

Send a BOM and we'll match part numbers, find alternates for long-lead or EOL lines, and return consolidated pricing and availability.

Hard-to-find components

Scarce, long-lead-time and discontinued parts are where we add the most value — cross-referencing packages, grades and last-time-buy stock.

How we work

Every request starts with what you actually need: a part number, a BOM, or a specification. We check Shenzhen availability, compare options on price and lead time, and confirm before you commit. You get a human reply — not an automated quote engine.
