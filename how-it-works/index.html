Process

How It Works

A clear, transparent sourcing process from your first requirement to delivery.

01 — Requirement

You send a BOM, part number, product specification or sourcing request. The more detail, the better we can match suppliers.

02 — Supplier Search

Our Shenzhen team identifies suitable manufacturers and suppliers, and communicates requirements locally on your behalf.

03 — Quote Comparison

We consolidate options and share pricing, availability, MOQ and lead time so you can compare and decide.

04 — Verification

We coordinate samples, product verification and pre-shipment checks to reduce risk before larger orders.

05 — Delivery

We consolidate products from multiple suppliers and arrange international delivery to your destination.

What we need from you

  • Part number
  • Product specification
  • Quantity
  • Target price (if available)
  • Required delivery date
  • Destination country
  • BOM or product files
