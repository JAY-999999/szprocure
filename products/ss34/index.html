Product Overview

The SS34 is a 3.0 A Schottky barrier rectifier in a surface-mount SMA package. It features low forward voltage drop and built-in strain relief, and is described by the manufacturer as ideal for automated surface-mount (pick-and-place) assembly. It is intended for rectification duties in low-voltage, high-frequency switching power supplies.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SS34

SS34 is a Schottky barrier rectifier in SMA (DO-214AC) rated 40 V and 3 A, with low forward voltage drop.

What is the reverse voltage rating of the SS34?

40 V maximum repetitive peak reverse voltage (VRRM).

What package does the SS34 use?

It is supplied in an SMA surface-mount package.

Is the SS34 lead-free / RoHS-capable?

The datasheet qualifies the device for Pb-free (lead-free) reflow assembly and the molding epoxy is rated UL 94V-0 flame retardant.
