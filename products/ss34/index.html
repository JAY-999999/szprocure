Product Overview

The SS34 is a 3.0 A Schottky barrier rectifier in a surface-mount SMA package. It features low forward voltage drop and built-in strain relief, and is described by the manufacturer as ideal for automated surface-mount (pick-and-place) assembly. It is intended for rectification duties in low-voltage, high-frequency switching power supplies.
