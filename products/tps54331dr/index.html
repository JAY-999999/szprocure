Product Overview

The TPS54331DR is a 28 V, 3 A non-synchronous step-down (buck) converter from Texas Instruments that integrates a low RDS(on) high-side MOSFET. It supports an adjustable output voltage down to 0.8 V and a fixed 570 kHz switching frequency, with a pulse-skipping Eco-mode feature that improves light-load efficiency and a typical 1 uA shutdown supply current suited to battery-powered applications. It is supplied in an 8-pin SOIC (D) package.
