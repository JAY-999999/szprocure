To ensure longevity and optimal brightness for this 0603 Red LED, you must limit the current. With a Forward Voltage (V_f) of 2.3V and a typical Forward Current (I_f) of 20mA:

What Is the Recommended Reflow Soldering Profile for the XL-1608SURC-06 SMD LED

This SMD component is designed for automated SMT assembly. The recommended Pb-Free reflow soldering peak temperature is 260°C for a maximum of 10 seconds. It is crucial to manage moisture sensitivity; once the vacuum seal is broken, the components should be mounted within 168 hours (MSL 3). For manual soldering, ensure the iron temperature does not exceed 300°C for more than 3 seconds to avoid damaging the internal die or the 0603 epoxy casing.;
