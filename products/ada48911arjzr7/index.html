Product Introduction

The ADA4891-1 (single), ADA4891-2 (dual), ADA4891-3 (triple), and ADA4891-4 (quad) are CMOS, high speed, high performance, low cost amplifiers with single-supply capability and an input voltage range that extends 300 mV below the negative supply rail.

Despite their low cost, the ADA4891 series delivers high performance and a wide range of features. The rail-to-rail output stage allows the output swing to come within 50 mV of either supply rail, providing maximum dynamic range.

The ADA4891 series amplifiers are well suited for imaging applications such as consumer video equipment, CCD buffers, and contact image sensor/buffers. Low distortion and fast settling time make these devices ideal for active filter applications.

The ADA4891-1/ADA4891-2/ADA4891-3/ADA4891-4 are available in multiple packages, rated for an extended temperature range of -40°C to +125°C.

Applications

  • - Automotive infotainment systems - Automotive driver assistance systems - Imaging - Consumer video equipment - Active filters - Coaxial cable drivers - Clock buffers - Photodiode preamplifiers - Contact image sensors and buffers
