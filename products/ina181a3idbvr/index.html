Product Introduction

The INA181, INA2181, and INA4181 (INAx181) current-sense amplifiers are designed for cost-optimized solutions. These devices are a series of bidirectional current-sense amplifiers (also known as current shunt monitors) that can sense the voltage drop across a current-sense resistor at common-mode voltages ranging from -0.2V to +26V, independent of the supply voltage. The INAx181 series integrates a matched resistor gain network with four fixed-gain device options: 20V/V, 50V/V, 100V/V, or 200V/V. This matched gain resistor network minimizes gain error and reduces temperature drift. These devices are powered by a single 2.7V to 5.5V supply. The single-channel INA181 consumes a maximum supply current of 260μA; the dual-channel INA2181 consumes a maximum supply current of 500μA, and the quad-channel INA4181 consumes a maximum supply current of 900μA. The INA181 is available in a 6-pin SOT - 23 package. The INA2181 is available in a 10-pin VSSOP package. The INA4181 is available in a 20-pin TSSOP package. All device options are specified for an extended operating temperature range of -40°C to +125°C.

Features

  • Common-mode range (VCM): -0.2V to +26V
  • High bandwidth: 350kHz (A1 device)
  • Offset voltage: ±150μV (max), VCM = 0V; ±500μV (max), VCM = 12V
  • Output slew rate: 2V/μs
  • Bidirectional current sensing accuracy: ±1% gain error (max)
  • 1μV/°C drift (max)
  • Gain options:
  • 20V/V (A1 device)
  • 50V/V (A2 device)
  • 100V/V (A3 device)
  • 200V/V (A4 device)
  • Quiescent current: 260μA max (INA181)
