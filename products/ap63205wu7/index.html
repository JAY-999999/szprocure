Frequently Asked Questions

What Are the Key Features of the DIODES AP63205WU-7 Synchronous Buck Converter?

The diodes ap63205wu-7 is a 2a synchronous buck converter with a wide input voltage range of 3.8v to 32v, featuring enhanced emi suppression, cycle-by-cycle current limiting, hiccup current limiting, and a low quiescent current of 22ua.

What Is the Operating Temperature Range for This Component?

The operating temperature range for this component is -40℃ to +85℃ at ambient temperature.

How Does the AP63205WU-7 Ensure EMI Suppression?

The ap63205wu-7 ensures emi suppression through frequency spread spectrum (fss) with a switching frequency jitter of ±6% and a proprietary gate driver scheme to resist switching node ringing.
