Product Introduction

STL160N10F8 is a 100V N-channel enhancement-mode power MOSFET designed with STripFET F8 technology, featuring an enhanced trench gate structure. It delivers an excellent figure of merit for very low on-state resistance while reducing internal capacitance and gate charge, enabling faster and more efficient switching.

Features

  • MSL1 rated
  • 175℃ maximum operating junction temperature
  • 100% avalanche tested
  • Low gate charge Qg

Applications

  • Server and telecom power supplies
  • Industrial battery management systems (BMS)
  • Power tools
  • Drones
