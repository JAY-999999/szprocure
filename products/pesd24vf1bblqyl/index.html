Product Introduction

Ultra-low capacitance bidirectional ESD protection diode in a DFN1006-2 (SOD882) leadless ultra-small SMD plastic package, designed to protect a single signal line against damage caused by ESD and other transient phenomena.

Features

  • Single-line bidirectional ESD protection
  • High reverse off-state voltage VRWM = 24 V
  • Ultra-low diode capacitance Cd = 0.28 pF
  • Ultra-compact plastic package 1.0x0.6x0.48 mm
  • ESD protection up to ±10 kV contact discharge per IEC 61000-4-2
  • AEC-Q101 qualified, recommended for automotive applications

Applications

  • - NFC antenna protection - High-speed data line protection
