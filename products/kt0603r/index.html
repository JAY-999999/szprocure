Frequently Asked Questions

What are the primary electro-optical and physical specifications of the KT-0603R LED?

The Hubei KENTO Elec KT-0603R is a red surface-mount LED in a standard 0603 (1608 Metric) package with dimensions of 1.6 mm × 0.8 mm × 0.6 mm. It features a transparent planar lens, is compatible with SMT automatic assembly and infrared reflow soldering processes, and complies with RoHS requirements. At a test current of 20 mA, the device provides a dominant wavelength of 615–630 nm, a forward voltage of 1.8–2.4 V, a typical viewing angle of 120°, and luminous intensity ranging from 145–300 mcd, depending on the selected bin.

What electrical considerations are required when driving the KT-0603R LED?

The KT-0603R has a forward voltage of 1.8–2.4 V at a forward current of 20 mA. The maximum continuous forward current is 25 mA, the peak forward current is 60 mA (0.1 ms pulse, 1/10 duty cycle), and the maximum reverse voltage is 5 V. The manufacturer recommends operating the LED at its rated current and using a current-limiting resistor in the circuit to prevent excessive current that could damage the device.

What are the operating limits and reliability considerations for the KT-0603R LED?

The KT-0603R has an operating temperature range of −40°C to +85°C and a storage temperature range of −40°C to +85°C. It is a RoHS-compliant surface-mount LED designed for automated assembly using standard lead-free reflow soldering processes. Long-term reliability depends on operating the device within the specified electrical and thermal limits and following the manufacturer's recommended handling, soldering, storage, and ESD protection practices.
