Frequently Asked Questions

What interface orientation, pin count, pitch spacing, and connector style define the SM02B-SRSS-TB(LF)(SN)?

The JST SM02B-SRSS-TB is a 1.0 mm pitch, 2-position, single-row shrouded header with a side-entry configuration. The datasheet includes the mechanical drawings and recommended PCB layout for the connector but does not explicitly describe the mounting method or assign the drawing dimensions as overall length, depth, or height.

What voltage, current, contact resistance, and insulation ratings apply to this header?

The connector is rated for 50 V AC/DC and 1.0 A AC/DC (AWG #28). Its electrical specifications include an initial contact resistance of 20 mΩ maximum, 40 mΩ maximum after environmental testing, 100 MΩ minimum insulation resistance, and a dielectric withstanding voltage of 500 VAC for one minute.

What materials and finishes are used in the SM02B-SRSS-TB(LF)(SN)?

The connector housing is made of PA (Polyamide/Nylon), UL94V-0, in a natural (ivory) color. The header contacts are copper alloy with copper undercoat and tin plating (reflow treatment). The solder tabs are made of brass with copper undercoat and tin plating (reflow treatment). The product is RoHS compliant.
