Frequently Asked Questions

What are the fundamental electrical specifications of the Samsung CL05A475MP5NRNC?

The Samsung CL05A475MP5NRNC is a multilayer ceramic capacitor (MLCC) with a nominal capacitance of 4.7μF, a rated voltage of 10V DC, and a ±20% capacitance tolerance (M). It uses an X5R Class II dielectric and comes in a 0402 (1005 metric) surface-mount package.

What are the package dimensions of the CL05A475MP5NRNC?

The CL05A475MP5NRNC is supplied in the standard 0402 (1005 metric) surface-mount package. According to Samsung's dimensions table, its body measures 1.00 ± 0.05 mm (L) × 0.50 ± 0.05 mm (W) × 0.50 ± 0.05 mm (T), with a termination width (BW) of 0.25 ± 0.10 mm. The product lineup for this specific part specifies a maximum thickness of 0.65 mm.

What dielectric material is used, and what is its operating temperature range?

The CL05A475MP5NRNC uses an X5R Class II ceramic dielectric. It is rated for an operating temperature range of −55°C to +85°C, with a capacitance change limited to ±15% over that temperature range.
