9.5mm×16mm×4.72mm 86A Package

A quad DC/DC step-down µModule (micromodule) regulator offering dual 12A and dual 5A output. Included in the package are the switching controllers, power FETs, inductors and support components. Operating over an input voltage range of 3.1V to 20V, it supports an output voltage range of 0.6V to 3.3V for two 12A channels and 0.6V to 5.5V for two 5A channels, each set by a single external resistor. Only bulk input and output capacitors are needed.

Fault protection features include overvoltage, overcurrent and overtemperature protection. It is offered in 9.5mm×16mm×4.72mm 86A package.
