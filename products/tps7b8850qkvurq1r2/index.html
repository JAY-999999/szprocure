Product Introduction

TPS7B88-Q1 is a low-dropout linear regulator designed for battery-connected automotive applications. With an input voltage range up to 40V, the device can withstand transients that may occur in automotive systems, such as load dump. Its quiescent current of only 17μA at light load makes it an excellent solution for powering always-on devices in standby systems, such as MCUs and CAN transceivers.

The device features advanced transient response, enabling the output to respond quickly to load or line variations (e.g., under cold-crank conditions). In addition, its innovative architecture minimizes output overshoot during recovery from voltage dropout. During normal operation, the device maintains ±1.15% DC accuracy across the full line, load, and temperature range.

The device is housed in a thermally enhanced package that efficiently conducts heat to the PCB.

Features

  • AEC-Q100 qualified for automotive applications: Temperature Grade 1: -40°C to +125°C, TA; Junction Temperature: -40°C to +150°C, TJ
  • Input voltage range: 3V to 40V (42V maximum)
  • Output voltage range: 3.3V and 5V (fixed)
  • Maximum output current: 500mA
  • Output voltage accuracy: ±1.15% (maximum)
  • Low dropout: 525mV at 450mA (maximum)
  • Low quiescent current: 17μA typical at light load
  • Excellent line transient response: ±2% deviation during cold start; ±2% deviation (VIN slew rate 1V/μs)
  • Stable with 2.2μF or larger capacitors
  • 3-pin TO-252 package: (RθJA): 30°C/W

Applications

  • Reconfigurable instrument cluster
  • Body Control Module (BCM)
  • Always-on battery-connected applications:
  • Automotive gateway
