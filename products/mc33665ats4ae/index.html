Product Introduction

MC33665A is a universal battery management communication gateway and Transformer Physical Layer (TPL) transceiver. The device forwards messages from different TPL (NXP's isolated daisy-chain protocol) ports via standard communication protocols. Standard communication protocols ensure compatibility with MCUs available on the market. The MC33665A is designed to implement one of two distinct functions: an enhanced TPL transceiver or a TPL-to-CAN (FD) gateway.

In the enhanced TPL transceiver variant, the device communicates directly with the MCU via SPI or UART. The device forwards messages from the integrated TPL ports.

In the CAN (FD) variant, the MC33665A connects to BMS devices via TPL. The device powers an external CAN transceiver, making it part of the CAN bus without requiring a microcontroller.

The MC33665A provides four isolated TPL daisy-chain ports for communication with other isolated BMS devices in the daisy chain. Each daisy-chain port supports capacitive and inductive isolation communication, ensuring interoperability with NXP battery management devices such as MC33771C, MC33772C, and MC33775A. The MC33665A supports communication protocols compliant with ASIL D and conforms to AEC-Q100 Grade 1.
