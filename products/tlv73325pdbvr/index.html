Product Introduction

The TLV733 series LDO regulators feature an ultra-small footprint and low quiescent current, capable of sourcing 300mA with excellent line and load transient performance. This series offers a typical accuracy of 1%.

The TLV733 series employs a capacitor-free architecture, ensuring stable operation without requiring input or output capacitors. Eliminating the output capacitor helps reduce solution size and eliminates inrush current during startup. However, the TLV733 series also operates stably with ceramic output capacitors. When an output capacitor is used, the TLV733 additionally provides foldback current control during device power-up and enable transitions — a feature particularly valuable for battery-powered applications.

The TLV733 features an active pulldown circuit that rapidly discharges the output load when disabled.

The TLV733 series is available in standard DBV (SOT-23) and DQN (X2SON) packages.

Features

  • Input voltage range: 1.4V to 5.5V
  • Stable operation with or without capacitors
  • Foldback overcurrent protection
  • Ultra-low dropout: 125mV at 300mA (3.3V_OUT)
  • Accuracy: 1% typical, 1.4% maximum
  • Low I_Q: 34μA
  • Fixed output voltage options: 1.0V to 3.3V
  • High PSRR: 50dB at 1kHz
  • Active output discharge

Applications

  • - Tablets - Smartphones - Laptop and desktop computers - Portable industrial and consumer products - WLAN and other PC add-in cards - Camera modules
