Product Overview

The B2B-XH-A(LF)(SN) is a 2-circuit, 2.5 mm-pitch through-hole shrouded board header from JST's XH connector series. It is a top-entry type with tin-plated phosphor-bronze contacts and a white PA66 (UL94V-0) housing, and is supplied RoHS-compliant as indicated by the (LF)(SN) label.

Frequently Asked Questions

What Is the Minimum Ordering Quantity (moq) and Packaging on Lcsc

LCSC offers no MOQ requirement for this part. The factory standard reel for this 2-circuit header is 1,000 pieces, with quantities available starting from 20 units.;

What Is the Operational Temperature Range for This Part

The header is rated for continuous operation from -25°C to +85°C, with that upper limit accounting for the temperature rise that occurs under normal electrical load.;

What Materials Are Used in Its Construction

The wafer (housing) is moulded from glass-filled PA66 plastic with a UL94V-0 flame-retardant rating. The header posts are brass with a copper undercoat and tin plating, providing reliable conductivity and corrosion resistance.;

What Is the Pitch and Mounting Type of This Component

This connector uses a 2.5mm pitch — the centre-to-centre distance between adjacent pins — and is designed for through-hole mounting on a printed circuit board.;

What Are the Electrical Ratings for This Connector Header

The B2B-XH-A(LF)(SN) is rated at 3A and 250V AC/DC, making it well suited for low-to-medium power signal connections on a PCB.

What is the pin pitch of the B2B-XH-A(LF)(SN)?

2.5 mm pitch, the standard XH series pitch.

Is this header RoHS compliant?

Yes. The datasheet marks it RoHS compliant via the (LF)(SN) label.

Through-hole or surface-mount?

It is a through-hole, top-entry shrouded header (radial-tape type).
