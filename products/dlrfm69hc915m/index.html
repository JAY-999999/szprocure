Product Introduction

DL-RFM69HC is a wireless transceiver module based on the Semtech SX1231 RF chip, operating across multiple license-free ISM bands including 315MHz, 433MHz, 868MHz, and 915MHz. All major RF communication parameters are programmable, with most configurable dynamically. The module offers the unique advantage of both narrowband and wideband communication modes, delivering high RF output power and channelized operation while optimized for low power consumption. Compliant with ETSI and FCC regulations.

Features

  • High sensitivity: -123dBm at 1.2kbps
  • High selectivity: 16-tap FIR channel filter
  • Robust front-end: IP3 = -18dBm, IP2 = +35dBm, 80dB blocking rejection, image-free frequency response
  • Low current: 16mA in receive mode, 100nA register retention current
  • Programmable output power: -18 to +17dBm in 1dB steps
  • Consistent RF performance across module voltage range
  • FSK bit rate up to 300kb/s
  • Fully integrated synthesizer with 61Hz resolution
  • FSK, GFSK, MSK, GMSK, and OOK modulation support
  • Built-in bit synchronizer with clock recovery
  • Sync word recognition, optional address and data filtering
  • Wireless packet support
  • RSSI listen mode support
  • 115dB+ dynamic range RSSI
  • Automatic RF detection with ultra-fast AFC
  • Packet engine with CRC-16, AES-128, 66-byte FIFO
  • Built-in temperature sensor
  • Module dimensions: 16×16mm

Applications

  • - Wireless automatic meter reading - Home and building automation - Industrial monitoring and control - Wireless sensor networks - Agricultural automation solutions
