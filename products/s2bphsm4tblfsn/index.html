Frequently Asked Questions

What are the key technical specifications of this JST connector header?

This is a 2-position wire-to-board connector header manufactured by JST (MPN: S2B-PH-SM4-TB(LF)(SN)). It belongs to the standard PH Series, featuring a 2mm pitch, a 1x2P single-row pin structure, and a Surface Mount, Right Angle (horizontal/side entry) configuration.

What materials are used in the construction and flame retardancy of this component?

The housing is molded from high-performance PA (Polyamide/Nylon) plastic with a Beige color and carries a UL94V-0 flame retardancy rating. The contacts are made of a durable Copper alloy finished with high-conductivity Tin plating.

Does this surface-mount connector include alignment or physical structural supports?

Yes, the layout features a Supplementary Auxiliary Solder Pin (solder tab/ retention clip) design. This enhances the physical mechanical bond to the PCB surface, helping prevent the connector from lifting or shearing off during cable plugging and unplugging.
