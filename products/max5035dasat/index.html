Product Introduction

Buck Switching Regulator IC Adjustable 1.25V~13.2V 1 Output 1A SOIC-8

Features

  • Wide 7.5V to 76V input voltage range
  • Fixed (3.3V, 5V, 12V) and adjustable (1.25V to 13.2V) versions
  • 1A output current
  • Up to 94% efficiency
  • Internal 0.4Ω high-side DMOS FET
  • 270μA quiescent current at no load, 10μA shutdown current
  • Internal frequency compensation
  • Fixed 125kHz switching frequency
  • Thermal shutdown and short-circuit current limiting
  • 8-pin SO and PDIP packages
  • Easy-to-use, high-efficiency, high-voltage step-down DC-DC converter with input voltage up to 76V
  • PWM converter with fixed 125kHz switching frequency at heavy load, automatically transitions to pulse-skip mode at light load
  • Includes UVLO, cycle-by-cycle current limiting, hiccup-mode output short-circuit protection
  • External shutdown with typical shutdown current of 10μA
  • Operating temperature range: -40°C to +125°C

Applications

  • - Automotive - Consumer Electronics - Industrial - Distributed Power
