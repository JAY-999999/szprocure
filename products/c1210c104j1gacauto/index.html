Product Introduction

KEMET's C0G dielectric automotive-grade series of chip capacitors is suitable for applications requiring reliable performance in harsh environments. Whether under the hood or inside the cabin, these devices demonstrate the importance and robustness of capacitors needed for critical and safety-critical automotive circuits. Given the potentially harsh environmental conditions, more stringent test protocols and inspection standards have been established for automotive-grade products. KEMET's automotive-grade series capacitors meet the rigorous AEC-Q200 certification requirements of the Automotive Electronics Council.

The C0G dielectric has a maximum operating temperature of 125°C and is classified as "stable." The Electronic Industries Alliance (EIA) classifies C0G dielectric as a Class I material. These components feature temperature compensation and are suitable for resonant circuit applications or applications requiring stable Q factor and capacitance characteristics. C0G capacitance exhibits no variation over time or voltage, with negligible capacitance change relative to ambient temperature. Over the temperature range of -55°C to +125°C, capacitance change is limited to ±30 ppm/°C.

  • Operating temperature range: -55°C to +125°C
  • Lead-free (Pb), RoHS and REACH compliant
  • Available in EIA 0402, 0603, 0805, 1206, 1210, 1812, and 2220 case sizes
  • DC voltage ratings: 10 V, 16 V, 25 V, 50 V, 100 V, 200 V, and 250 V
  • Capacitance range: 0.5 pF to 0.47 μF
  • Capacitance tolerances: ±0.10 pF, ±0.25 pF, ±0.5 pF, ±1%, ±2%, ±5%, ±10%, and ±20%
  • No piezoelectric noise
  • Ultra-low ESR and ESL
  • High thermal stability
  • High ripple current capability
  • Preferred capacitor solution for line frequency and MHz range applications
  • No capacitance change under rated DC voltage
  • Negligible capacitance variation with temperature from -55°C to +125°C
  • No capacitance aging over time
  • Non-polarized, simplifying mounting concerns
  • 100% pure matte tin termination for excellent solderability

Applications
