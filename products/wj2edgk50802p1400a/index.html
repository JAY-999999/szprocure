Frequently Asked Questions

What are the rated voltage and current specifications of the WJ2EDGK-5.08-02P-14-00A?

What are the pitch and pin configuration of the WJ2EDGK-5.08-02P-14-00A?

The WJ2EDGK-5.08-02P-14-00A features a 5.08 mm pitch and a 2-position (2P) configuration. According to the manufacturer's part-number coding, "02P" indicates the number of poles. It is part of the WJ2EDGK pluggable terminal block series.

What wire sizes and screw specifications does the WJ2EDGK-5.08-02P-14-00A support?
