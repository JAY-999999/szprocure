Frequently Asked Questions

Is This Component Environmentally Compliant?

Yes, this ceramic capacitor is fully RoHS compliant, meaning it meets strict international environmental regulations limiting the use of hazardous substances like lead.

What Is the Physical Package Size of This Component?

This capacitor comes in an 0402 surface-mount (SMD) package format, which measures approximately 1.0mm × 0.5mm (0.04 inches × 0.02 inches). Its tiny footprint makes it perfect for space-constrained PCB designs.

What Does the "x7r" Temperature Coefficient Mean?

X7R is a EIA temperature coefficient code indicating that the capacitor has an operating temperature range of -55°C to +125°C, with a maximum capacitance variation of ±15% over that entire range. This makes it highly stable for standard decoupling and filtering applications.
