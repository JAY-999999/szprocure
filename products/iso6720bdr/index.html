Product Introduction

The ISO672xB devices are high-performance dual-channel digital isolators that offer an isolation rating of 3000 VRMS (D package) in accordance with UL 1577, making them well-suited for cost-sensitive applications with such requirements. These devices are also certified by VDE, TUV, CSA, and CQC. While isolating CMOS or LVCMOS digital I/Os, the ISO672xB devices provide high electromagnetic immunity and low emissions, along with low power consumption. The logic input and output buffers of each isolation channel are isolated by TI's dual-capacitor silicon dioxide (SiO2) insulation gate. The ISO6720B device features two in-phase isolation channels. The ISO6721B device has two reverse-phase isolation channels. In the event of input power or signal loss, devices without the suffix F default to a high output, while those with the suffix F default to a low output. When used in conjunction with isolated power supplies, these devices help prevent data buses such as UART, SPI, RS - 485, RS - 232, and CAN from damaging sensitive circuits. Thanks to the innovative chip design and wiring technology, the electromagnetic compatibility of the ISO672xB devices is significantly enhanced, which can mitigate system-level ESD, EFT, and surge issues and meet radiation standards. The ISO672xB series of devices are available in an 8-pin SOIC narrow-body (D) package and are a pin-to-pin upgrade from previous generations of devices.

Features

  • Documentation for functional safety system design: ISO6720, ISO6721
  • 50Mbps data rate
  • Robust isolation barrier: long operational lifetime at 450V<sub>RMS</sub> working voltage; isolation rating up to 3000V<sub>RMS</sub>, typical CMTI of ±150kV/μs
  • Wide supply voltage range: 1.71V to 1.89V and 2.25V to 5.5V
  • 1.71V to 5.5V level translation
  • Default output high (ISO672xB) and low (ISO672xFB) options
  • Wide temperature range: –40°C to 125°C
  • Typical per-channel current of 1.8mA at 1Mbps
  • Low propagation delay: 11ns (typical)
  • Excellent EMC performance: system-level ESD, EFT, and surge immunity across the isolation barrier with ±8kV IEC 61000-4-2 contact discharge protection; low EMI
  • Narrow SOIC (D-8) package
  • Safety-related certifications: DIN VDE V 0884-11:2017-01, UL 1577 component recognition program, IEC 62368-1, IEC 61010-1, IEC 60601-1, GB 4943.1-2011
