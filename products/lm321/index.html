Product Overview

The LM321 brings performance and economy to low power systems. With a high unity gain frequency and a specified 0.4V/µs slew rate, the quiescent current is only 430µA/amplifier (5V). The input common-mode voltage range includes ground, so the device operates in both single-supply and dual-supply applications, and it can comfortably drive large capacitive loads.
