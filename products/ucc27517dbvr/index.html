Product Introduction

Texas Instruments UCC27517DBVR - 11.25 V

Features

  • Low-cost gate driver offering a high-quality alternative to discrete NPN and PNP solutions
  • Symmetric 4A peak source and 4A peak sink drive capability
  • Short propagation delay (13ns typical)
  • Short rise and fall times (9ns and 7ns typical, respectively)
  • 4.5V to 18V single-supply range
  • Output held low during VDD UVLO (ensures glitch-free operation during power-up and power-down)
  • TTL and CMOS compatible input logic thresholds (independent of supply voltage)
  • Hysteretic logic thresholds for high noise immunity
  • Dual-input design (selectable inverting (IN− pin) or non-inverting (IN+ pin) driver configuration)
  • Unused input pin configurable for enable/disable function
  • Output held low when input pins are floating
  • Input pin absolute maximum voltage levels not restricted by VDD pin bias supply voltage
  • Operating temperature range: −40°C to 140°C
  • Available in 5-pin DBV (SOT-23) and 6-pin DRS (3mm × 3mm WSON with exposed thermal pad) package options

Applications

  • - Switched-mode power supplies - DC-DC converters - Companion gate driver devices for digital power controllers - Solar energy, motor control, UPS - Gate drivers for emerging wide-bandgap power devices (e.g., GaN)
