Product Introduction

A high-precision, ultra-low noise, low-distortion, fully differential input/output amplifier optimized for 3V, single-supply operation. The output common-mode voltage is independent of the input common-mode voltage and can be adjusted by applying a voltage to the VOCM pin. A dedicated internal common-mode feedback path provides accurate output phase balance and reduces even-order harmonics. This makes it ideal for level-shifting ground-referenced signals to drive differential-input, single-supply ADCs.

The outputs feature rail-to-rail swing capability and can source and sink up to 85mA of current. In addition to low-distortion performance, it offers a low input-referred voltage noise of 3nV/√Hz. The device maintains its performance at supply voltages as low as 2.375V. It draws only 13.5mA of supply current and features a hardware shutdown function that reduces current consumption to 300μA.

Available in 8-pin MSOP or 8-pin DFN packages.
