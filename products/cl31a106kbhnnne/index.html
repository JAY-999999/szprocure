What Are the Practical Implications of the X5R Dielectric in This Capacitor

The X5R classification indicates a Class II ceramic dielectric rated from -55°C to +85°C with capacitance change within ±15% of nominal across that range. Unlike X7R (rated to +125°C), X5R capacitors are not recommended for designs requiring stable capacitance above 85°C. Additionally, X5R and all Class II ceramics exhibit DC voltage derating: the effective capacitance of this 10µF / 50V device will be lower when operated at a significant fraction of its 50V rated voltage.;
