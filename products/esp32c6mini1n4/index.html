Product Introduction

Espressif Systems ESP32-C6-MINI-1-N4 - GPIO;SPI;UART;I2C;I2S - 3.30 V

Features

  • Integrated ESP32-C6FH4 chip, RISC-V 32-bit single-core processor, up to 160MHz clock frequency, with 320KB ROM, 512KB HP SRAM, 16KB LP SRAM, and 4MB in-package flash
  • Wi-Fi supports 2.4GHz band, IEEE 802.11ax compliant with OFDMA, MU-MIMO, beamforming, and more; backward compatible with IEEE 802.11b/g/n, supports 20MHz and 40MHz bandwidth, data rate up to 150Mbps, multiple operating modes supported
  • Bluetooth LE certified with Bluetooth 5.3, supports Bluetooth mesh, high-power mode (20dBm), multiple data rates (125Kbps to 2Mbps), and extended advertising; Wi-Fi and Bluetooth coexistence supported
  • IEEE 802.15.4-2015 compliant, operates at 2.4GHz, data rate 250Kbps, supports Thread 1.3 and Zigbee 3.0
  • Integrated 40MHz crystal oscillator; onboard PCB antenna or external antenna connector options available
  • Rich peripherals including GPIO, SPI, UART, I2C, I2S, RMT, LED PWM, USB Serial/JTAG controller, MCPWM, SDIO 2.0 slave controller, GDMA, TWAI controller, ADC, temperature sensor, timers, and more
  • Operating voltage range 3.0~3.6V, operating temperature -40~85°C (85°C version) or -40~105°C (105°C version)

Applications

  • - Smart Home - Industrial Automation - Healthcare - Consumer Electronics - Smart Agriculture
