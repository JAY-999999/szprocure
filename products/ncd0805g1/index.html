Frequently Asked Questions

How Should I Drive and Mount This LED During Assembly?

Mount the LED using standard SMT reflow soldering processes according to recommended 0805 footprint profiles. To safely drive the LED, use a current-limiting resistor calculated for a nominal forward current of 20mA based on your supply voltage and the LED forward voltage range of 2.5V to 3.6V.

What Are the Main Technical Specifications of the NCD0805G1?

The NATIONSTAR NCD0805G1 is an 0805 green SMD LED with a peak wavelength of 520nm, luminous intensity of 400mcd, and a 130-degree viewing angle. It operates at a forward current of 20mA with a forward voltage between 2.5V and 3.6V across an operating temperature range of -30°C to +85°C.

What Are the Primary Application Scenarios for This Green LED?

This green SMD LED is ideal for status indication and backlighting across various electronic devices. Common applications include household appliance indicator lights, mobile phone keypad backlights, automotive dashboard displays, network communication equipment, and general SMT electronic products requiring high-visibility visual feedback in a compact footprint.
