Frequently Asked Questions

What forward voltage range, continuous drive current, and electrical operating limits govern the YLED0603G LED?

The YONGYUTAI YLED0603G is an emerald green surface-mount LED with a rated forward current (IF) of 25 mA. Its forward voltage (VF) is specified as 2.6 V to 3.2 V at IF = 5 mA. The LED has a maximum reverse voltage (VR) of 5 V and is rated for an operating temperature range of -40°C to +85°C.

What luminous intensity, dominant wavelength, and viewing angle characterize the YLED0603G LED?

The YLED0603G provides a luminous intensity of 173 to 358 mcd at IF = 5 mA. It has a wavelength range of 510 nm to 531 nm at IF = 20 mA and features a 120° viewing angle (2θ1/2) for wide-angle illumination.

Which package dimensions and physical configuration define the YLED0603G?

The YLED0603G is supplied in a 0603 surface-mount package with body dimensions of 1.6 mm × 0.8 mm × 0.6 mm. It is a two-terminal device, with Pin 1 as the anode (+) and Pin 2 as the cathode (-).
