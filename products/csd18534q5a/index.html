Product Introduction

This 7.8 mΩ, 60 V, SON 5 × 6 mm NexFET power MOSFET is designed to minimize losses in power conversion applications.

Features

  • Ultra-Low Qg and Qgd
  • Low Thermal Resistance Avalanche Rated
  • Logic Level
  • Pb Free Terminal Plating
  • RoHS Compliant Halogen Free SON 5 mm × 6 mm Plastic Package
