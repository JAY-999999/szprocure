Product Overview

The W25Q64JVSSIQ is a Winbond 64M-bit (8 MByte) Serial Flash memory from the SpiFlash family, supporting Standard, Dual and Quad SPI with clock rates up to 133 MHz (266 MHz equivalent in Dual I/O, 532 MHz in Quad I/O). It operates from a 2.7-3.6 V supply, draws as little as 1 uA in power-down, and is organized as 32,768 programmable 256-byte pages with 4 KB sector and 32/64 KB block erase. The SSIQ variant is an 8-pin 208-mil SOIC suited to code shadowing, XIP execution and data storage.
