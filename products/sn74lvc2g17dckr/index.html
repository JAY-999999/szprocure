Product Introduction

Dual Schmitt-Trigger Buffer

Features

  • Schmitt-Trigger inputs provide hysteresis
  • Available in the NanoFree™ Package
  • Supports 5-V VCC Operation
  • Inputs Accept Voltages to 5.5 V
  • Max tpd of 5.4 ns at 3.3 V
  • Low Power Consumption, 10-μA Max ICC
  • ±24-mA Output Drive at 3.3 V
  • Typical VOLP (Output Ground Bounce) < 0.8 V at VCC = 3.3 V, TA = 25℃
  • Typical VOHV (Output VOH Undershoot) >2 V at VCC = 3.3 V, TA = 25℃
  • Ioff Supports Live Insertion, Partial-Power-Down Mode Operation and Back-Drive Protection
  • Latch-Up Performance Exceeds 100 mA Per JESD 78, Class II
  • ESD Protection Exceeds JESD 22
  • 2000-V Human-Body Model
  • 1000-V Charged-Device Model
