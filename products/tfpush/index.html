Frequently Asked Questions

What is the primary function of the SHOU HAN TF PUSH connector

The SHOU HAN TF PUSH is a T-Flash (TF) card connector designed for memory card applications. It is a connector manufactured by SHOU HAN Technology and is intended for mounting on a printed circuit board (PCB). Its mechanical, electrical, and environmental characteristics are defined in the product specification.;

What are the physical mounting characteristics of this connector

The connector's mechanical dimensions and recommended PCB layout are provided in the product drawing included with the specification. The drawing includes the recommended PCB footprint and mounting features for board installation. The specification refers users to the customer drawing for the detailed dimensions and PCB layout but does not explicitly classify the connector as surface-mount (SMT) or through-hole.;

How does the card insertion mechanism work

The connector is identified as TF PUSH, and the mechanical drawing labels the operating positions as "PUSH IN" and "PUSH OUT," indicating a push-operated card insertion and ejection mechanism. The specification does not provide additional narrative describing the internal latch or spring mechanism beyond the mechanical drawing.;

What are the key electrical characteristics of this connector

The connector has an initial contact resistance of 40 mΩ maximum, an insulation resistance of 1000 MΩ minimum, and a dielectric withstand rating of 500 VAC for 1 minute with a maximum leakage current of 1.0 mA. It is also rated for 5,000 mating/unmating cycles, with contact resistance not exceeding 100 mΩ after the durability test.;

What environmental and assembly requirements does this connector meet

The connector uses materials that comply with RoHS requirements. Recommended storage conditions are −25°C to +85°C with relative humidity of 80% or less, and products stored for more than six months should be re-qualified before use. The datasheet also provides wave soldering and reflow soldering profiles for automated assembly
