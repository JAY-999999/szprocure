Frequently Asked Questions

What Is the Pinout Compatibility of the EVISUN B0505S-1WR3 with Other Standard Isolation Modules?

The B0505S-1WR3 features an industry-standard SIP package pinout, making it physically and electrically compatible with most 5V to 5V 1W isolated DC-DC converters on the market that follow the same footprint. However, always verify your PCB layout against the provided dimension sheet before replacing an existing component.

What Are the Primary Application Scenarios for This DC-DC Converter?

This module is ideally suited for distributed power systems requiring electrical isolation from the input supply. Common application scenarios include pure digital circuits, general low-frequency analog circuits, relay driver circuits, and data switching networks where noise reduction or ground-loop elimination is necessary.

What Are the Key Technical Specifications Regarding Input and Output?

The EVISUN B0505S-1WR3 operates within a fixed input voltage range of 4.5V to 5.5V (5V nominal) and provides an isolated, non-regulated 5V single-channel output. It delivers a maximum output current of 200mA (1W power), features an 86% conversion efficiency, and offers an isolation voltage of 1500VDC/min.
