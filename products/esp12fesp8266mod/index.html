Frequently Asked Questions

What Communication Interfaces Are Compatible With the ESP-12F Module?

The Ai-Thinker ESP-12F(ESP8266MOD) module supports UART, I2C, and SPI communication interfaces, making it highly versatile for connecting with various microcontrollers, sensors, and peripheral hardware. It operates on a standard 3.3V power supply, so logic level shifters are required when interfacing with 5V systems to prevent hardware damage.

What Are Suitable Alternatives or Upgrades for the ESP-12F Module?

If you require more processing power or additional GPIOs, ESP32 modules serve as excellent direct upgrades over the ESP-12F(ESP8266MOD). For drop-in ESP8266 replacements, modules like the ESP-12E offer similar form factors and pinouts, though the ESP-12F provides improved antenna layout and enhanced RF signal stability for better wireless transmission performance.

What Are the Key Technical Specifications of the ESP-12F Module?

The Ai-Thinker ESP-12F(ESP8266MOD) features a 2.4GHz Wi-Fi transceiver based on the ESP8266 chip, operating at 3.3V. It delivers an output power of 16dBm with -90dBm sensitivity via an on-board PCB antenna. Operating current is 170mA during transmission and 120mA during reception, across a temperature range of -40°C to +85°C.
