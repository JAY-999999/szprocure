Frequently Asked Questions

What Are the Key Technical Specifications of This Ceramic Capacitor?

This is a Surface Mount Device (SMD/SMT) Multilayer Ceramic Capacitor (MLCC) manufactured by Samsung Electro-Mechanics (MPN: CL05A105KP5NNNC). It features a capacitance value of 1µF with a ±10% tolerance, a 10V maximum voltage rating, and is enclosed in a miniature 0402 package.

What Dielectric Material and Temperature Characteristics Does This MLCC Use?

This capacitor uses an X5R dielectric material. It is designed to operate reliably within a temperature range of -55°C to +85°C, maintaining a maximum capacitance variation of ±15% across this span.

Does This 0402 Capacitor Have a Strict Moisture Sensitivity Level (MSL) Rating?

No, this MLCC carries an MSL 1 (Moisture Sensitivity Level 1) rating. This indicates an unlimited floor life under ambient conditions (≤30°C/85% RH) and means it does not require specialized dry-bag storage or a pre-assembly baking process prior to standard infrared reflow soldering.
