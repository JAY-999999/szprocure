Frequently Asked Questions

What capacitance value, DC working voltage, tolerance, and maximum leakage current define the UWT1H221MNL1GS?

The Nichicon UWT1H221MNL1GS is a chip-type aluminum electrolytic capacitor with a nominal capacitance of 220µF and a rated working voltage of 50V DC. It has a standard capacitance tolerance of ±20% (M). After 2 minutes of applying the rated voltage at 20°C, the maximum DC leakage current is 110µA (0.01CV). The maximum dissipation factor (tan δ) is 0.14 at 120Hz and 20°C.

What temperature limits and load-life endurance ratings apply to the UWT1H221MNL1GS?

As part of Nichicon's UWT 105°C chip-type aluminum electrolytic capacitor series, the UWT1H221MNL1GS operates over a temperature range of −55°C to +105°C. The series is rated for an endurance of 1,000 hours with the rated voltage applied at 105°C, after which it meets the specified endurance characteristics.

What AC ripple current rating does the UWT1H221MNL1GS support?

The UWT1H221MNL1GS has a rated ripple current of 220mArms at 120Hz and 105°C. The datasheet also provides ripple current frequency coefficients, including a coefficient of 1.50 at 10kHz or higher. This coefficient can be used to calculate the allowable ripple current at higher frequencies (e.g., 220 × 1.50 ≈ 330mArms); however, the datasheet does not explicitly specify 330mArms as a rated ripple current.

Which physical dimensions and package characteristics define the UWT1H221MNL1GS?

The UWT1H221MNL1GS is a chip-type aluminum electrolytic capacitor with a 10.0mm diameter × 10.0mm height (Ø10 × 10mm) package. According to the part numbering system, "NL" is the size code corresponding to this package size, while "M" denotes the ±20% capacitance tolerance. The package dimensions include a 10.3mm × 10.3mm plastic platform, and the UWT series is AEC-Q200 qualified.
