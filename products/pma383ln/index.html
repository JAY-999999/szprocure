Product Introduction

The PMA3-83LN+ is a PHEMT based wideband, low noise MMIC amplifier with a unique combination of low noise, high IP3, and flat gain over wideband making it ideal for sensitive, high-dynamic-range receiver applications. This design operates on a single 5V or 6V supply, is well matched for 50Ω and comes in a tiny, low profile package (3×3×0.89mm), accommodating dense circuit board layouts.

Features

  • Low Noise figure, 1.3 dB at 2 GHz
  • High IP3, 35 dBm typ. at 2 GHz
  • High Pout, P1dB 20.7 dBm typ. at 2 GHz and 6V
  • Excellent Gain flatness, ±0.9 dB over 0.5 to 7 GHz and 6V

Applications

  • - WiFi - WLAN - UMTS - LTE - WiMAX - S-band Radar - C-band Satcom
