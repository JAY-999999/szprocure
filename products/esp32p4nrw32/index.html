Frequently Asked Questions

What Applications Benefit Most From the Integrated Hardware Accelerators?

The dual-core RISC-V architecture combined with hardware H.264 video encoding, JPEG processing, ISP, and Pixel Processing Accelerator makes it ideal for camera video streaming, speech recognition, smart POS terminals, industrial automation, and edge AI vision tasks needing real-time multimedia processing.

What Are the Core Power and Processing Specifications?

Powered by a 32-bit dual-core RISC-V HP processor and an LP single-core processor up to 40 MHz, it operates at 3.0V–3.6V across -40℃ to +85℃. On-chip memory includes 768 KB HP L2MEM, 32 KB LP SRAM, dual 12-bit ADCs, and 55 programmable GPIOs in a QFN-104 package.

How Do You Connect High-Speed External Memory and Peripherals?

You can interface external memory through high-speed memory controllers with two-level caching support. Peripherals connect via built-in high-speed USB 2.0 OTG, 100 Mbps Ethernet MAC, SD/MMC 3.0 host, 3 TWAI (CAN) controllers, and multiple SPI, UART, I2C, and I2S interfaces routed through 55 configurable GPIO pins.
