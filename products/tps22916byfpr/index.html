Product Introduction

TPS22916xx is a compact single-channel load switch that uses a low-leakage P-channel MOSFET to minimize power loss. The advanced gate control design supports operating voltages as low as 1V while further reducing on-resistance and power loss.

Multiple timing options accommodate a variety of system load conditions. For high-capacitance loads, the slow turn-on timing of the C version minimizes inrush current. For low-capacitance loads, the fast timing of the B version reduces the required wait time.

Switch conduction state is controlled by a digital input that connects directly to low-voltage control signals. The device is available in both active-high and active-low (L) versions. At initial power-up, the device uses a smart pull-down resistor to keep the ON pin from floating until system timing control is complete. Once the ON pin is intentionally driven high (≥VH), the smart pull-down resistor is disconnected, preventing unnecessary power loss.

The TPS22916xx is offered in a space-saving 4-pin wafer chip-scale package (WCSP) (YFP) measuring 0.78mm × 0.78mm with 0.4mm pitch and 0.5mm height. The device operates over a temperature range of –40°C to +85°C.

Features

  • Input Voltage Range (VIN): 1V to 5.5V
  • Maximum Continuous Current (IMAX): 2A
  • On-Resistance (RON):
  • 5V VIN = 60mΩ (typical), 100mΩ (maximum at 85°C)
  • 1.8V VIN = 100mΩ (typical), 150mΩ (maximum at 85°C)
  • 1V VIN = 200mΩ (typical), 325mΩ (maximum at 85°C)
  • Ultra-Low Quiescent Current:
  • On-State (IQ): 0.5μA (typical), 1μA (maximum)
  • Shutdown State (ISD): 10nA (typical), 100nA (maximum)
  • TPS22916BL/CL/CNL (ISD): 100nA (typical), 300nA (maximum)
  • ON Pin Intelligent Pull-Down Resistor (RPD):
  • ON ≥ VH (ION): 10nA (maximum)
  • ON ≤ VL (RPD): 750kΩ (typical)
  • Slow Slew Rate (C Version) for Inrush Current Limiting:
  • 5V Turn-On Time (tON): 1400μs at 5mV/μs
  • 1.8V Turn-On Time (tON): 3000μs at 1mV/μs
  • 1V Turn-On Time (tON): 6500μs at 0.3mV/μs
  • Fast Slew Rate (B Version) for Reduced Latency:
  • 5V Turn-On Time (tON): 115μs at 57mV/μs
  • 1.8V Turn-On Time (tON): 250μs at 12mV/μs
  • 1V Turn-On Time (tON): 510μs at 3.3mV/μs
  • Always-On True Reverse Current Blocking (RCB)
  • Quick Output Discharge (QOD): 150Ω (typical) (N version without QOD)
  • Active-Low Enable Option (L version)
