What Are the Key Electrical Parameters of the CL10A106KP8NNNC, and Is 10V Sufficient for Typical 3.3V and 5V Decoupling Applications

The CL10A106KP8NNNC is a 10uF ceramic capacitor rated at 10V with an X5R dielectric in a 0603 package. For a 3.3V power rail, the 10V rating provides a comfortable safety margin of approximately 3x. For a 5V rail, the margin is 2x, which is generally acceptable for most digital systems. However, designers should account for DC bias derating: at 5V, the effective capacitance of an X5R-dielectric part may reduce significantly. Verify actual capacitance under DC bias conditions before finalizing the design for 5V applications.;

What does the X5R dielectric classification mean for the CL10A106KP8NNNC, and over what temperature range is its capacitance guaranteed

The X5R designation follows the EIA standard: X = lower temperature limit of -55°C, 5 = upper limit of +85°C, and R = capacitance change within ±15% across that range. This makes the CL10A106KP8NNNC suitable for general-purpose decoupling, bulk energy storage, and power filtering from -55°C to +85°C. X5R offers better capacitance stability than Y5V but lower stability than C0G/NP0. It is not recommended for precision timing or resonant circuits where tight capacitance tolerance is required over the full operating temperature range.;
