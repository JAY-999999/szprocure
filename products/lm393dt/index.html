The LM393DT consists of two independent low-voltage comparators designed to operate from a single supply over a wide voltage range (2 V to 36 V) or from split supplies (±1 V to ±18 V). The input common-mode voltage range includes ground even when operated from a single supply; supply current is very low (0.45 mA typ) and independent of supply voltage. Outputs are open-collector and compatible with TTL, DTL, ECL, MOS and CMOS logic. The DT suffix denotes the SO-8 package.
