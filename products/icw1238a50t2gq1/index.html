Product Introduction

The ICW1238-Q1 series is a high-voltage (up to 40V), low-power LDO regulator fabricated using CMOS technology. It delivers up to 1A of output current with a quiescent current of only 1.6uA. The device consists of a voltage reference generator, error amplifier, current foldback circuit, phase compensation circuit, and transistor driver circuit. The ICW1238-Q1 is a high-performance LDO regulator designed for automotive applications, with the entire product family certified to AEC-Q100 Grade 1 standards.

Features

  • Ultra-low quiescent current: 1.6uA
  • Maximum input voltage: 40V
  • Output voltage accuracy: ±2%
  • Maximum output current: 500mA
  • Dropout voltage: 10mV@Iout=10mA
  • Temperature stability: ±45ppm/°C
  • ON/OFF Logic = Enable High
  • Protection circuits: over-temperature protection, current limiting, foldback function
  • Output capacitor: low ESR, ceramic capacitor compatible
  • Temperature grade 1: -40°C to +125°C

Applications

  • Body Control Module (BCM)
  • EV Battery Management System
  • Audio Head Unit
  • Smart Cockpit Power Management
  • Automotive Electric Sunroof Power Management
