Product Overview

The CL10B104KB8NNNC is a Samsung multi-layer ceramic capacitor (MLCC) in a 0603 (1608 metric) surface-mount case. Decoded from the ordering code, it is a 100 nF plusminus 10% X7R dielectric part rated at 50 V with a 0.80 mm body thickness. X7R dielectrics are specified for -55 degC to +125 degC operation; Samsung's CL-series catalog lists this part for use in DC-DC converters, LCD/LED, memory modules and consumer/handheld electronics.
