Product Introduction

WS2812B - 2020 is an intelligent control LED light source, its exterior adopts the latest Molding packaging technology, the control circuit and RGB chips are integrated in a package of 2020 component. Its internal include intelligent digital port data latch and signal reshaping amplification drive circuit. Also include a precision internal oscillator and voltage programmable constant current control part, which achieves highly consistent color effect.

The data transfer protocol use single NZR communication mode. After the pixel power - on reset, the DIN port receive data from controller, the first pixel collect initial 24bit data then sent to the internal data latch, the other data which reshaping by the internal signal reshaping amplification circuit sent to the next cascade pixel through the DO port. After transmission for each pixel, the signal to reduce 24bit. pixel adopt auto reshaping transmit technology, making the pixel cascade number is not limited the signal transmission, only depend on the speed of signal transmission.

RESET time >280μs, it won't cause wrong reset while interruption, it supports the lower frequency and inexpensive MCU. Refresh Frequency updates to 2KHz, Low Frame Frequency and No Flicker appear in HD Video Camera, it improve excellent display effect. LED with low driving voltage, environmental protection and energy saving, high brightness, scattering angle is large, good consistency, low power, long life and other advantages. The control chip integrated in LED above becoming more simple circuit, small volume, convenient installation.

Frequently Asked Questions

For What Types of Applications Is the WS2812B-2020 Best Suited, and Are There Any Assembly Restrictions to Be Aware Of?

The WS2812B-2020 is optimally suited for applications requiring high-density RGB pixel arrays in space-constrained designs, including flexible LED matrices, wearable electronics, miniature display panels, and consumer lighting fixtures where the standard 5x5mm WS2812B would be too large. The 2.2x2mm package is fully compatible with automated SMT pick-and-place assembly using standard vacuum nozzles sized for 0402–0603 components. The component is RoHS compliant. Its CNHTS code is 8541410090, confirming its classification as a semiconductor light-emitting device for international trade and customs compliance.

Does the WS2812B-2020 Include an Integrated Control IC, and What Data Protocol Does It Use for Color Control?

Yes. The WS2812B-2020 is described as an intelligent control LED integrated light source, meaning the control IC, RGB LED chip, and decoupling capacitor are all integrated within the 2.2x2mm SMD package. It uses Worldsemi's proprietary single-wire NZR serial communication protocol, which is fully compatible with the standard WS2812B signal format. This allows the WS2812B-2020 to be driven by any microcontroller or FPGA that supports the WS2812 protocol, enabling cascaded daisy-chain LED strip topologies with a single data line carrying 24-bit GRB color data per pixel.
