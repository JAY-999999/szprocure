Product Introduction

Full-speed/Low-speed 2.5kV USB Digital Isolator

Features

  • USB 2.0 compatible
  • Low-speed and full-speed data rates: 1.5 Mbps and 12 Mbps
  • Bidirectional communication
  • V_sus operating voltage: 4.5 V to 5.5 V
  • Upstream supply current: 7 mA (max, 1.5 Mbps)
  • Upstream supply current: 8 mA (max, 12 Mbps)
  • Upstream idle current: 2.3 mA (max)
  • Upstream short-circuit protection
  • Class 3A contact ESD performance per ANSI/ESD STM5.1-2007
  • Operating temperature up to: 105 °C
  • High common-mode transient immunity: >25 kV/μs
  • 16-pin wide-body SOIC package
  • RoHS compliant
  • Qualified for automotive applications
  • Safety and regulatory approvals — UL recognition: per UL 1577, 1-minute 2,500 V rms
  • CSA Component Acceptance Notice #5A
  • IEC 60950-1: 600 V rms (basic)
  • VDE certificate DIN V VDE V 0884-10 (VDE V 0884-10):2006-12
  • V_IORM = 560 V peak

Applications

  • USB peripheral isolation
  • Isolated USB hub
