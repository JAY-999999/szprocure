Product Introduction

- H-bridge circuit topology with integrated N-channel and P-channel power MOSFETs, optimized for driving inductive loads such as coils and motors.

- Integrated over-temperature protection: shuts off load current when internal temperature exceeds the set threshold (typical 150°C); resumes normal operation when junction temperature drops back to the preset level (typical 130°C). Note: no short-circuit protection is provided — output-to-GND, output-to-supply, or output-to-output shorts may cause device damage. Avoid short-circuit conditions during use, or implement current-limiting measures to prevent such damage.

- Logic supply VCC and power supply VDD are fully independent internally. Route them separately in layout; do NOT connect VCC and VDD directly together, as the high voltage on VDD may damage the VCC rail.

Features

  • Low standby current (< 0.1uA)
  • Operating voltage range: 2V - 8.6V
  • Maximum continuous output current: 1.2A (VDD = 6.5V)
  • Maximum peak output current: 2.5A (VDD = 6.5V)
  • Low on-resistance: 0.5Ω at 1200mA output current; 0.35Ω at 200mA output current
  • Low input current — logic inputs INA\INB with integrated 15K pull-down resistors to GND
  • Built-in thermal shutdown (TSD) with hysteresis
  • ESD rating: 3KV (HBM)
