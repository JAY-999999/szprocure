Frequently Asked Questions

What are the fundamental resistance ratings, tolerance limits, and temperature coefficients of the RC0603FR-0747KL?

The YAGEO RC0603FR-0747KL is a general-purpose thick film surface-mount resistor with a nominal resistance of 47 kΩ. In the part number, "F" indicates a ±1% resistance tolerance, while "R" denotes paper tape and reel packaging. The resistor has a Temperature Coefficient of Resistance (TCR) of ±100 ppm/°C.

Which maximum power dissipation limits and working voltage thresholds constrain circuit design?

The RC0603FR-0747KL is rated for 0.1 W (100 mW, 1/10 W) at 70°C. It has a maximum continuous working voltage of 75 V, a maximum overload voltage of 150 V, and a dielectric withstanding voltage of 150 V.

What standardized package dimensions and board space requirements define this component?

The resistor is supplied in a 0603 surface-mount package with nominal body dimensions of 1.60 ± 0.10 mm × 0.80 ± 0.10 mm and a height of 0.45 ± 0.10 mm. Its external terminations feature a nickel barrier with a matte tin finish for lead-free assembly.
