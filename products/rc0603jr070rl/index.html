Frequently Asked Questions

What are the primary electrical specifications and physical characteristics of the YAGEO RC0603JR-070RL resistor?

It is supplied in a standard 0603 surface-mount package with nominal dimensions of 1.60 mm × 0.80 mm × 0.45 mm. For jumper applications, the RC0603 series specifies a maximum resistance of less than 50 mΩ, a rated current of 1.0 A, and a maximum current of 2.0 A.

What is the purpose of a 0Ω resistor such as the RC0603JR-070RL?

The RC0603JR-070RL is a 0Ω jumper resistor within the YAGEO RC_L Series. According to the datasheet, jumper devices use a conductor in place of the resistive element and are specified with a maximum resistance below 50 mΩ. The component shares the same standard 0603 surface-mount package as other RC_L Series resistors, allowing it to be assembled using standard SMT manufacturing processes.

How do temperature and power limits affect the RC0603JR-070RL?

The datasheet also specifies qualification testing, including endurance, high-temperature exposure, humidity, thermal shock, board flex, and solderability, with acceptance criteria defined for jumper devices.
