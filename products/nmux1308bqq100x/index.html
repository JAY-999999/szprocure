Product Introduction

The NMUX1308-Q100 is a general-purpose CMOS bidirectional 8-channel analog switch operating from 1.5 V to 5.5 V. The device is dual-source compatible with existing 4851 and 4051 devices. The NMUX1308-Q100 features extended digital logic thresholds for compatibility with 1.8 V systems without voltage translation. Analog signal pins include one common input/output (Z) and eight individual input/outputs (Y0 through Y7). All analog signal pins are bidirectional and support voltages from GND to VCC. All analog signal pins integrate injection current control circuitry. This control circuitry isolates overvoltage transients on unconnected analog signal pins from the connected analog signal path, maintaining measurement accuracy. Additionally, this integrated design eliminates the need for external overvoltage clamping components such as resistor-diode networks. Four control signal pins are provided (S0, S1, S2, and E̅). S0, S1, and S2 select the analog channel connected between Z and Yn. E̅ overrides S0, S1, and S2 to disconnect all analog channels. The control signal pins support 1.8 V logic thresholds across all operating voltages. Furthermore, these pins are 5.5 V tolerant, supporting operating voltages up to 5.5 V regardless of supply voltage. This product is AEC-Q100 Grade 1 qualified for automotive applications.

Features

  • AEC-Q100 (Grade 1) automotive qualification
  • Operating temperature range: -40℃ to +85℃ and -40℃ to +125℃
  • SP8T-Z function
  • Wide operating voltage range: 1.5 V to 5.5 V
  • Rail-to-rail operation on analog signal pins
  • Injection current control
  • 1.8 V digital logic threshold
  • Digital pins compatible with 1.8 V logic threshold across the full VCC range
  • Low-voltage GPIO compatible without level-shifting pull-up devices
  • Ioff circuitry
  • Greater flexibility for power sequencing considerations
  • Blocks backflow between isolated power rail and any biased digital/analog inputs when VCC = 0 V
  • Prevents back-powering to VCC from any biased digital/analog inputs when VCC = 0 V
  • Maintains high-impedance state of analog switches when VCC = 0 V
  • 5.5 V overvoltage-tolerant digital inputs
  • Supports switching of 5.5 V digital signals across the full VCC operating range
  • No pull-down translation required when switching threshold is met
  • Pin-compatible with industry-standard 4051 and 4851 analog switch products
  • ESD protection: HBM: compliant with ANSI/ESDA/JEDEC JS-001 Class 2, exceeds 2000 V; CDM: compliant with ANSI/ESDA/JEDEC JS-002 Class C2b, exceeds 750 V
  • DHVQFN package with solderable side-wing leads supporting automated optical inspection (AOI) of solder joints

Applications

  • Body control module
  • Battery management system
  • Automotive head unit
