Frequently Asked Questions

Can the CL31B106KBHNNNE Be Used as a Drop-In Replacement for Other 10uF 50V X7R 1206 Ceramic Capacitors From Murata or TDK?

Yes. The CL31B106KBHNNNE uses the industry-standard 1206 (3216 Metric) SMD footprint. It is functionally compatible with equivalents such as Murata GRM31CR71H106KA12L and TDK C3216X7R1H106K160AC, provided the DC bias derating characteristics and rated temperature ranges are consistent with the application. Samsung Electro-Mechanics is a Tier-1 global passive component manufacturer, ensuring consistent lot-to-lot quality. Verify the datasheet capacitance-vs-voltage derating curve before substitution in precision timing or resonant circuits where the effective capacitance under bias is critical.

What Does the X7R Dielectric Specification Mean for the CL31B106KBHNNNE, and Is It Suitable for Industrial Temperature Applications?

The X7R classification means this capacitor maintains its capacitance within ±15% over the temperature range of -55°C to +125°C. This wide operating range makes the CL31B106KBHNNNE suitable for industrial and automotive-adjacent designs where ambient temperatures may exceed the +85°C limit of X5R-grade capacitors. At 10uF / 50V, the part is particularly well-suited for power rail filtering and hold-up applications in harsh environments. The ±10% tolerance (K-grade) is standard for X7R bulk decoupling. DC bias derating is inherent to ceramic capacitors of this type — the effective capacitance will decrease under applied DC voltage, which should be evaluated from the datasheet.

What Are the Key Parameters of the CL31B106KBHNNNE, and Why Would a Designer Choose a 1206 Package for a 10uF 50V Ceramic Capacitor?

The CL31B106KBHNNNE is a 10uF ±10% ceramic capacitor rated at 50V with an X7R dielectric, housed in a 1206 (3216 Metric) package from Samsung Electro-Mechanics. The 50V rating is significantly higher than typical 10uF MLCC options in smaller packages (which are often limited to 10V or 16V). A 1206 package is selected when higher voltage ratings are required — a 50V rating in 10uF is difficult to achieve in 0603 or 0805 packages. This makes the CL31B106KBHNNNE a strong choice for bulk decoupling on 12V power rails, input filter capacitors in DC-DC converters, and hold-up capacitors in industrial power supply designs.
