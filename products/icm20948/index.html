Product Introduction

9-Axis MEMS MotionTracking Device - QFN-24(3x3) IMUs (Inertial Measurement Units) RoHS

Features

  • Lowest Power 9- Axis Device at 2.5 mW
  • 3- Axis Gyroscope with Programmable FSR of ±250 dps, ±500 dps, ±1000 dps, and ±2000 dps
  • 3- Axis Accelerometer with Programmable FSR of ±2g, ±4g, ±8g, and ±16g
  • 3- Axis Compass with a wide range to ±4900μT
  • Onboard Digital Motion Processor (DMP)
  • Android support
  • Auxiliary I²C interface for external sensors
  • On- Chip 16- bit ADCs and Programmable Filters
  • 7 MHz SPI or 400kHz Fast Mode I²C
  • Digital- output temperature sensor
  • VDD operating range of 1.71V to 3.6V
  • MEMS structure hermetically sealed and bonded at wafer level
  • RoHS and Green compliant

Applications

  • - SMARTphones and Tablets - Wearable Sensors - IoT Applications
