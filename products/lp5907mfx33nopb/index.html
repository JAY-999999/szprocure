Product Introduction

250mA, low noise, high PSRR, ultra-low dropout regulator with low IQ and enable functionality

Features

  • Input voltage range: 2.2V to 5.5V
  • Output voltage range: 1.2V to 4.5V
  • Output current: 250mA
  • Stable performance with 1μF ceramic input and output capacitors
  • No noise bypass capacitor required
  • Supports remote output capacitor placement
  • Thermal overload and short-circuit protection
  • Operating junction temperature range: -40°C to 125°C
  • Low output voltage noise: <10μV RMS
  • PSRR: 82dB at 1kHz
  • Output voltage tolerance: ±2%
  • Near-zero IQ (disabled): <1μA
  • Ultra-low IQ (enabled): 12μA
  • Start-up time: 80μs
  • Low dropout: 120mV (typical)

Applications

  • Mobile phones
  • PDA handheld terminals
  • Wireless LAN devices
