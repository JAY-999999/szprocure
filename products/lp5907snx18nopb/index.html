Product Introduction

The LP5907 is a linear regulator capable of delivering 250mA output current. This device is specifically designed for RF and analog circuits, meeting their requirements for low noise, high PSRR, low quiescent current, and low line or load transient response. The LP5907 employs innovative design techniques to deliver excellent noise performance without a noise bypass capacitor, while supporting remote placement of the output capacitor. This device is designed to work with 1μF input and 1μF output ceramic capacitors (no separate noise bypass capacitor required). Fixed output voltage ranges from 1.20V to 4.50V in 25mV increments.

Features

  • Input voltage range: 2.2V to 5.5V
  • Output voltage range: 1.2V to 4.5V
  • Output current: 250mA
  • Stable performance with 1μF ceramic input and output capacitors
  • No noise bypass capacitor required
  • Supports remote output capacitor placement
  • Thermal overload protection and short-circuit protection
  • Operating junction temperature range: -40°C to 125°C
  • Low output voltage noise: <10μV RMS
  • PSRR: 82dB at 1kHz
  • Output voltage tolerance: ±2%
  • Near-zero IQ (disabled): <1μA
  • Ultra-low IQ (enabled): 12μA
  • Startup time: 80μs
  • Low dropout: 120mV (typical)

Applications

  • - Mobile phones - PDA handheld terminals - Wireless LAN devices
