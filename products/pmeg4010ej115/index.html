Product Introduction

Planar Schottky barrier rectifiers with an integrated guard ring for stress protection, encapsulated in a SOD323F (SC - 90) small Surface - Mounted Device (SMD) plastic package.

Features

  • Forward current: IF ≤ 1 A
  • Reverse voltage: VR ≤ 40 V
  • Very low forward voltage

Applications

  • - Low voltage rectification - High efficiency DC-to-DC conversion - Switch mode power supply - Reverse polarity protection - Low power consumption applications
