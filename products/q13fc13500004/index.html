Product Introduction

The Q13FC13500004 is a 32.768 kHz tuning-fork quartz crystal from Epson in a small surface-mount package, with a nominal load capacitance of 12.5 pF. Its low drive level (0.1-0.5 microW) and -40 to +85 degC operating range suit low-power timing-reference use.

Applications

  • Real-time clock (RTC) timing reference
  • Low-power microcontroller clock source
  • Battery-powered / wearable timing circuits (0.1-0.5 microW drive level)

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the core electrical and physical specifications of the EPSON Q13FC13500004 crystal

The EPSON Q13FC13500004 is a 32.768 kHz crystal unit in Epson's FC-135 series. It has a nominal load capacitance of 12.5 pF, a frequency tolerance of ±20 ppm at +25°C (measured at a 0.1 µW drive level), and an operating temperature range of -40°C to +85°C. The crystal has external dimensions of 3.2 ±0.1 mm × 1.5 ±0.1 mm × 0.8 ±0.1 mm, a maximum drive level of 0.5 µW, a maximum motional resistance of 70 kΩ, and a first-year frequency aging specification of ±3 ppm at +25°C.;

What types of circuits is the EPSON Q13FC13500004 designed for

The Q13FC13500004 is a 32.768 kHz crystal unit designed to pair with oscillator circuits built for a 12.5 pF load capacitance and a drive level of 0.1 µW typical (0.5 µW maximum). Designers should confirm their oscillator or RTC circuit is specified for these values before use.;

What are the temperature and frequency stability characteristics of the EPSON Q13FC13500004

The crystal is specified for operation from -40°C to +85°C. Its frequency tolerance is ±20 ppm at +25°C, with a turnover temperature of 25°C (typical) and a parabolic coefficient of -0.04 × 10⁻⁶/°C². The specified first-year frequency aging is ±3 ppm at +25°C, and the maximum drive level is 0.5 µW.;
