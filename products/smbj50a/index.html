Product Introduction

Littelfuse SMBJ5.0A

Features

  • 600 W peak pulse power capability at 10/1000 μs waveform, repetition rate (duty cycle): 0.01%
  • Surface mount package for optimized board space
  • Low dynamic resistance
  • Typical failure mode: short circuit due to overvoltage or overcurrent
  • IEC-61000-4-2 ESD 30 kV (air discharge), 30 kV (contact discharge)
  • ESD protection for data lines per IEC 61000-4-2
  • Whisker tested per JEDEC JESD201A Tables 4a and 4c, passes Level 1 and Level 2
  • VBR @ TJ = VBR @ 25
  • Cx(1 + αTx(TJ - 25)) (αT: temperature coefficient, typical value 0.1%)
  • UL-recognized compound, meets V-0 flammability rating
  • Matte tin lead-free plating
  • Halogen-free and RoHS compliant
  • Lead-free E3 denotes second-level interconnect lead-free, terminal finish material: tin (Sn) (IPC/JEDEC J-STD-609A.01)
  • UL 497B recognized as isolating loop circuit protector
  • Products manufactured outside China are available (parts with E suffix). Refer to the "Ordering and Packaging Options" section for details
