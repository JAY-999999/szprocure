What Are Common Applications for the ADS1115IDGSR

The ADS1115IDGSR is well-suited for sensor signal conditioning, data acquisition systems, and battery voltage monitoring. Its 16-bit resolution and adjustable gain amplifier make it ideal for precision measurements in embedded systems, IoT devices, and industrial control applications where accurate analog signal conversion is required. Engineers often choose this IC when they need low-power, high-resolution ADC functionality in space-constrained designs.;

What Are the Key Specifications and Features of the ADS1115IDGSR

The ADS1115IDGSR is a 16-bit analog-to-digital converter (ADC) from Texas Instruments that converts analog signals to digital format. It features a programmable gain amplifier (PGA), I²C serial interface, and supports up to 860 samples per second. The device offers four single-ended or two differential input channels, making it flexible for various measurement applications. It operates on 2V to 5.5V supply and comes in a compact VSSOP-10 surface-mount package.
