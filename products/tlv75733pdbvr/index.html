Frequently Asked Questions

What fixed output voltage, maximum load current, and input operating limits define the TLV75733PDBVR LDO regulator

The Texas Instruments TLV75733PDBVR is a low-dropout linear regulator that provides a fixed 3.3 V output with ±1% output accuracy. It operates from an input voltage range of 1.45 V to 5.5 V and delivers up to 1.0 A of continuous output current while providing good line and load transient performance for powering microcontrollers and other low-voltage digital circuits.;

What maximum dropout voltage, supply quiescent current, and power-saving features govern this power regulator

The device features a low quiescent current of 25 µA typical (40 µA maximum over the operating temperature range) to minimize standby power consumption. At a 1.0 A load with a 3.3 V output, the maximum dropout voltage is 425 mV (or 475 mV across the full junction temperature range), helping maintain regulation when the input voltage is close to the output voltage.;

Which physical package dimensions, lead layout codes, and part-number suffixes specify this component

The orderable part number TLV75733PDBVR identifies a 3.3 V fixed-output member of the TLV757P regulator family. The "P" suffix indicates active output discharge, "DBV" specifies the 5-pin SOT-23 surface-mount package, and "R" denotes reel packaging (3,000 pieces). The SOT-23 package has nominal body dimensions of 2.90 mm × 1.60 mm with a 0.95 mm pin pitch.;

What active protections, internal soft-start mechanisms, and output capacitor requirements ensure safe operation

The TLV75733PDBVR integrates active output discharge, thermal shutdown, undervoltage lockout (UVLO), internal foldback current limiting, and built-in soft-start for reliable operation and short-circuit protection. It supports a junction temperature range of −40°C to +125°C and is stable with a 0.47 µF or larger ceramic output capacitor, with 1 µF commonly recommended for typical applications.;

What packaging options and factory reel quantities apply to the TLV75733PDBVR

The "R" suffix indicates reel packaging, with a standard quantity of 3,000 devices per reel. The device is supplied in a 5-pin SOT-23 (DBV) surface-mount package suitable for automated PCB assembly and is qualified for lead-free reflow soldering with a peak body temperature of 260°C.
