What are the key electrical ratings and contact configuration of the Songle SRD-12VDC-SL-C relay

The Songle SRD-12VDC-SL-C is a PCB-mounted electromechanical relay with a 12V DC coil and a Single Pole Double Throw (SPDT / 1 Form C) contact configuration. The SRD series is available with 7A, 10A, and 15A contact ratings. For the 10A version, the contacts are rated for 10A at 250VAC. The 12V coil has a typical power consumption of 0.36W, corresponding to a coil current of approximately 30mA and a coil resistance of approximately 400Ω at 20°C.;

What are the coil operating characteristics of the SRD-12VDC-SL-C

The SRD-12VDC-SL-C uses a 12V DC coil with a typical power consumption of 0.36W. At the rated voltage, the coil current is approximately 30mA, and the coil resistance is approximately 400Ω at 20°C, as specified in the datasheet.;
