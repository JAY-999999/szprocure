Product Overview

The ADXL345BCCZ-RL7 is a small, thin, ultralow-power 3-axis digital accelerometer capable of measuring acceleration up to +/- 16 g with 13-bit resolution. Output data is formatted as 16-bit two's complement and accessed over an SPI (3- or 4-wire) or I2C digital interface. It consumes as little as 23 uA in measurement mode and 0.1 uA in standby at VS = 2.5 V, making it well suited to mobile and battery-powered devices. It is offered in a 14-pin LCC package.
