Frequently Asked Questions

What voltage rating, current capacity, and wire gauge ranges govern the JST XHP-2 housing?

The JST XHP-2 wire-to-board socket housing is rated for a maximum voltage of 250V AC/DC and a continuous current capacity of up to 3A AC/DC when used with AWG #22 conductors. It accepts compatible crimp socket contacts, including the SXH-001T-P0.6 and SXH-002T-P0.6 series, supporting wire sizes from AWG #30 to #22 (0.05–0.33 mm², depending on contact type) with insulation outer diameters ranging from 0.9 mm to 1.9 mm.

Which pin pitch, position count, housing material, and flammability ratings define the XHP-2?

The XHP-2 features a 2.5 mm pitch in a single-row, 2-position configuration. It is molded from natural white PA6 (Nylon 6) with a UL94V-0 flammability rating. The datasheet lists the housing dimensions for the XHP-2 as A = 2.5 mm, B = 5.7 mm, and C = 7.3 mm.

Which PCB headers and crimp contacts pair directly with the XHP-2 housing?

The XHP-2 receptacle housing is compatible with the 2-circuit JST XH-series through-hole PCB headers, including the B2B-XH-A top-entry and S2B-XH-A side-entry headers. It accepts SXH-001T-P0.6 and SXH-002T-P0.6 crimp contacts for wire termination.
