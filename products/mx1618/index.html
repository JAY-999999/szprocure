Product Introduction

MX1618 is an H-bridge brushed DC motor driver IC designed for multi-cell battery-powered systems.

● The typical application of MX1618 is with a 12V/16V supply voltage input, driving 1 motor. The maximum rated voltage of the IC must not exceed 20V, and the supply voltage must not fall below 4V during normal operation.

● MX1618 features low on-resistance and provides a continuous current output capability of 1.8A. When the load current exceeds 3.6A, the IC enters overcurrent protection.

● The internal low-voltage circuitry is powered by a built-in LDO. When the IC detects no input signal for more than 100ms, it automatically enters standby mode, in which the quiescent current consumption is less than 1uA.

● This product is equipped with a dedicated current sense output pin (Isense). The current sensing ratio of the sense output circuit is 2500:1; the output sense current can be used by an MCU or other peripheral control circuits to regulate the output current.

Features

  • Wide operating voltage range: 4V~16V, supporting 5~8 AA battery applications and 2~3 cell Li-ion battery applications
  • Ultra-low standby current: quiescent current less than 1uA with no signal input
  • Integrated low on-resistance power transistor: on-resistance less than 0.5Ω (TJ=150℃), continuous current output up to 1.8A in ESOP8 package
  • Built-in current sensing circuit
  • Multiple protections: built-in over-temperature protection at 160℃, built-in over-current protection with current limit at 3.6A
  • Pin-compatible with Series-6 motor driver ICs
  • ESD rating greater than 4Kv

Applications

  • - Electric toys - Robots - Electronic locks - Magnetic latching relays
