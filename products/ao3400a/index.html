Product Overview

The AO3400A is an N-channel MOSFET in a SOT-23 package, built with advanced trench technology. It offers low on-resistance (RDS(ON) below 17 mOhm at VGS = 10 V), low gate charge, and can be turned on with gate voltages as low as 2.5 V. The manufacturer states it is suitable for use as a load switch or in PWM applications.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is AO3400A used for

AO3400A is an N-channel MOSFET (30 V, 5.7 A, 48 mΩ RDS(on)) in SOT-23, commonly used for load switching and DC-DC conversion.

What is the maximum continuous drain current of the AO3400A?

5.8 A at 25 degC ambient (4.9 A at 70 degC), per the Absolute Maximum Ratings table.

What gate drive voltage is required to turn it on?

It can operate with gate voltage as low as 2.5 V; RDS(ON) is rated at VGS = 4.5 V and 10 V. Gate threshold is 0.7-1.4 V.

What package does the AO3400A come in?

SOT-23 (order code 'UMW AO3400A', 3000 pcs tape-and-reel).
