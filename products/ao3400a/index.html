Product Introduction

AO3400A combines advanced trench MOSFET technology with low-resistance packaging to achieve extremely low RDS(ON). This device is suitable for use as a load switch or in PWM applications.

Applications

  • Load switch
  • PWM applications (e.g., motor / LED driving)
  • Low-voltage / battery-powered circuits where gate drive is limited (operates from 2.5 V gate)
