Frequently Asked Questions

Is the W5500 Compatible With 5V Microcontrollers

Yes. The W5500 operates on a 3.3V supply but its I/O pins are 5V tolerant, so it can interface directly with 5V microcontrollers (such as traditional Arduino boards) without external logic level shifters;

What Should I Look for in an Alternative to the W5500

A drop-in replacement should offer an integrated Ethernet MAC and PHY, a standard SPI host interface, and a similar hardwired TCP/IP stack to avoid extra software overhead. Note that alternative chips may use different pinouts and require updated software libraries, since the W5500's register map and SPI frame format are specific to WIZnet's implementation.;

In What Application Scenarios Is the W5500 Typically Used

The W5500 is commonly used in home network devices (set-top boxes, PVRs, digital media adapters), serial-to-Ethernet and parallel-to-Ethernet converters, USB-to-Ethernet and GPIO-to-Ethernet adapters, security systems (DVRs, network cameras, kiosks), factory and building automation, medical monitoring equipment, and embedded servers.;
