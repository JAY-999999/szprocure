Frequently Asked Questions

How Does the 525nm Dominant Wavelength of the KT-0805G Define Its Visual Appearance?

A dominant wavelength of 525nm corresponds to the emerald green portion of the visible spectrum. This is distinct from yellow-green LEDs (typically 570nm) and blue-green LEDs. The 525nm designation means the KT-0805G emits a visually pure green, making it well suited for status indicator applications where green-channel discrimination is important (e.g., multi-color indicator arrays alongside red and amber LEDs). The forward voltage range of 2.6V to 3.1V is characteristic of high-efficiency InGaN green LEDs.

Can the KT-0805G Be Used as a Drop-in Replacement for Other 0805 Green LEDs?

The 0805 (2.0mm × 1.25mm) package is an industry-standard SMD footprint. Other 0805 green LEDs with a similar dominant wavelength near 525nm and a forward voltage in the 2.6V to 3.1V range may serve as footprint-compatible replacements. However, luminous intensity, viewing angle, and exact Vf characteristics vary by manufacturer and part number. Always verify the current limiting resistor calculation against the target LED's Vf and desired current using the KT-0805G datasheet values before substituting.
