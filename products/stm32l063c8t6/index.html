Product Introduction

Ultra-low-power 32-bit MCU based on Arm Cortex-M0+ core, featuring 64KB Flash, 8KB SRAM, 2KB EEPROM, with LCD, USB, ADC, DAC, and AES support.

Features

  • Ultra-low-power platform: supply voltage 1.65V to 3.6V, operating temperature range -40℃ to 125℃; standby mode current 0.27µA (2 wake-up pins), Stop mode current 0.4µA (16 wake-up lines), Stop mode + RTC + 8KB RAM retention current 0.8µA, run mode current 88µA/MHz; wake-up from RAM 3.5µs, wake-up from Flash 5µs
  • Core: Arm 32-bit Cortex-M0+ (with MPU), frequency range 32kHz to 32MHz, 0.95 DMIPS/MHz
  • Memory: 64KB Flash with ECC, 8KB RAM, 2KB data EEPROM with ECC, 20-byte backup registers, sector-level read/write protection
  • Up to 51 fast I/Os (45 are 5V-tolerant)
  • Reset and power management: ultra-safe low-power BOR with 5 selectable thresholds; ultra-low-power POR/PDR; programmable voltage detector (PVD)
  • Clock sources: 1 to 25MHz crystal oscillator; 32kHz RTC oscillator (with calibration); high-speed internal 16MHz factory-trimmed RC (±1%); internal low-power 37kHz RC; internal multi-speed low-power 65kHz to 4.2MHz RC; internal 48MHz self-calibrating RC (for USB); CPU clock PLL
  • Pre-programmed bootloader: USART, SPI
  • Development support: serial wire debug
  • LCD driver: up to 8×28 segments, contrast adjustment, blinking mode, on-board step-up converter
  • Analog peripherals: 12-bit ADC (1.14 Msps, up to 16 channels, down to 1.65V operation); 12-bit single-channel DAC (with output buffer, down to 1.8V operation); 2 ultra-low-power comparators (window mode and wake-up capability, down to 1.65V operation); up to 24 capacitive sensing channels (touch keys, linear and rotary touch sensors)
  • 7-channel DMA controller: ADC, SPI, I2C, USART, DAC, timers, AES
  • 8 peripheral communication interfaces: 1× USB 2.0 (crystal-less, battery charge detection and LPM); 2× USART (ISO7816, IrDA); 1× UART (low-power); up to 4× SPI (16 Mbits/s); 2× I2C (SMBus/PMBus)
  • 9 timers: 1× 16-bit timer (up to 4 channels), 2× 16-bit timers (up to 2 channels), 1× 16-bit ultra-low-power timer, 1× SysTick timer, 1× RTC, 1× 16-bit basic timer (for DAC), 2× watchdogs (independent/window)
  • Others: CRC calculation unit, 96-bit ID, true RNG and firewall protection, AES 128-bit hardware encryption engine; all packages are ECOPACK2
