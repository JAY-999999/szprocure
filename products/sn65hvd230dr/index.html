Frequently Asked Questions

What primary supply voltage levels, signaling rates, and node density limits define the SN65HVD230DR transceiver?

The Texas Instruments SN65HVD230DR is a high-speed CAN (Controller Area Network) transceiver designed to operate from a single 3.3 V supply, with a recommended operating range of 3.0 V to 3.6 V. It is compatible with the ISO 11898 high-speed CAN physical layer standard and supports signaling rates of up to 1 Mbps. Its high input impedance allows up to 120 nodes to be connected to a single CAN bus.

Which package type is available for the SN65HVD230DR?

The SN65HVD230DR is supplied in an 8-pin SOIC (D) surface-mount package. This package is intended for standard SOIC PCB assembly and is available under the ordering options listed in the Package Option Addendum. For detailed mechanical dimensions and PCB land-pattern recommendations, refer to the corresponding TI package outline drawing.

How do the slope control pin and low-power standby mode operate on the SN65HVD230DR?

The RS (Pin 8) pin supports three operating modes: high-speed mode, slope-control mode, and low-power standby mode. Connecting RS directly to ground enables maximum switching speed. Connecting a 10 kΩ to 100 kΩ resistor to ground adjusts the output slew rate to reduce electromagnetic interference (EMI). Driving RS to a logic-high level places the device into a low-current standby mode, in which the driver is disabled while the receiver remains active. The standby supply current is typically 370 μA.
