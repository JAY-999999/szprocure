Product Introduction

TI TLV9064IPWR

Features

  • Rail-to-rail input and output
  • Low input offset voltage: ±0.3mV
  • Unity-gain bandwidth: 10MHz
  • Low wideband noise: 10nV/√Hz
  • Low input bias current: 0.5pA
  • Low quiescent current: 538μA
  • Unity-gain stable
  • Internal RFI and EMI filters
  • Operates from supply voltages as low as 1.8V
  • Resistive open-loop output impedance for easier stabilization with higher capacitive loads
  • Shutdown version: TLV906xS
  • Extended temperature range: -40°C to +125°C
  • TLV900x devices feature a shutdown mode that allows the amplifier to switch to standby mode with typical current consumption below 1μA
  • TLV906xS series simplifies system design with unity-gain stability, integrated RFI and EMI suppression filters, and no phase inversion under overdrive conditions
  • Miniature packages (e.g., SOT-553 and WSON) and industry-standard packages (e.g., SOIC, MSOP, SOT-23, and TSSOP) available for all channel configurations (single, dual, and quad)
