What Guidelines Should Be Followed for PCB Footprint Layout and PCB Assembly?

Follow standard IPC 0402 footprint land patterns with appropriate solder pad dimensions to prevent tombstoning. Ensure proper DC bias de-rating is considered during design, and maintain standard IPC-A-610 reflow profile temperatures during assembly to prevent thermal shock and ceramic cracking.
