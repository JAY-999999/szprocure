Frequently Asked Questions

What are the primary charging specifications of the TOP POWER ASIC TP4056-42-ESOP8?

The TOP POWER ASIC TP4056-42-ESOP8 is a standalone linear battery charger designed for a single lithium-ion or lithium-polymer cell. The device operates from an input voltage range of 4V to 8V and uses a constant-current/constant-voltage (CC/CV) charging algorithm. It provides a fixed 4.2V charge voltage, and the charge current is programmed with a single external resistor and can be configured up to 1A.

Which batteries and power sources are compatible with the TP4056-42-ESOP8?

The TP4056-42-ESOP8 is designed for charging a single lithium-ion or lithium-polymer battery. It is designed to operate from a USB power source or a wall adapter, making it suitable for single-cell battery charging applications. The device provides a fixed 4.2V charge voltage and supports programmable charging current up to 1A using an external resistor.

What protection and charging-management features are built into the TP4056-42-ESOP8?

The TP4056-42-ESOP8 automatically progresses through trickle-charge, constant-current, and constant-voltage charging stages based on battery condition. Charging terminates automatically when the charge current drops to one-tenth of the programmed value after reaching the final charge voltage. The device also supports automatic recharge when the battery voltage falls below the recharge threshold. Additional features include thermal regulation, under-voltage lockout (UVLO), cell temperature monitoring through the TEMP pin, C/10 charge termination, soft-start, and two charge-status output pins (CHRG and STDBY).
