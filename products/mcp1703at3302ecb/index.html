Product Introduction

Microchip Technology MCP1703AT-3302E/CB - Fixed - 3.30 V - 0.250 A

Features

  • Reduced Ground Current During Dropout
  • Faster Startup Time
  • 2.0 μA Typical Quiescent Current
  • Input Operating Voltage Range: 2.7V to 16.0V
  • 250 mA Output Current for Output Voltages ≥2.5V
  • 200 mA Output Current for Output Voltages <2.5V
  • Low Dropout Voltage, 625 mV Typical @ 250 mA for V_R = 2.8V
  • 0.4% Typical Output Voltage Tolerance
  • Standard Output Voltage Options: - 1.2V, 1.5V, 1.8V, 2.5V, 2.8V, 3.0V, 3.3V, 4.0V, 5.0V
  • Output Voltage Range: 1.2V to 5.5V in 0.1V Increments (50 mV increments available upon request)
  • A/D Friendly Voltage Options: 2.05V, 3.07V, 4.1V
  • Stable with 1.0 μF to 22 μF Ceramic Output Capacitance
  • Short-Circuit Protection
  • Overtemperature Protection

Applications
