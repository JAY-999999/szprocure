Product Introduction

Programmable Resolution 1-Wire Digital Thermometer

Features

  • Unique 1- Wire Interface Requires Only One Port Pin for Communication
  • Reduce Component Count with Integrated Temperature Sensor and EEPROM
  • Measures Temperatures from -55℃ to +125℃ (-67°F to +257°F)
  • ±0.5℃ Accuracy from -10℃ to +85℃
  • Programmable Resolution from 9 Bits to 12 Bits
  • No External Components Required
  • Parasitic Power Mode Requires Only 2 Pins for Operation (DQ and GND)
  • Simplifies Distributed Temperature- Sensing Applications with Multidrop Capability
  • Each Device Has a Unique 64- Bit Serial Code Stored in On- Board ROM
  • Flexible User- Definable Nonvolatile (NV) Alarm Settings with Alarm Search Command
  • Identifies Devices with Temperatures Outside Programed Limits
  • Available in 8- Pin SO (150 mils), 8- Pin sSOP, and 3- Pin TO- 92 Packages

Applications

  • Thermostatic Controls
  • Industrial Systems
  • Consumer Products
  • Thermometers
  • Thermally Sensitive Systems

Frequently Asked Questions

What power supply boundaries, temperature sensing limits, and measurement accuracy specifications govern the DS18B20+?

The DS18B20+ is a digital temperature sensor operating across an external supply voltage (VDD) range of 3.0V to 5.5V. It measures temperatures from -55°C to +125°C with an accuracy of ±0.5°C over the -10°C to +85°C range. During temperature conversions, the active supply current is 1.5mA maximum at 5V, while standby current is 750nA typical.

How does resolution selection alter temperature measurement precision and conversion timing in the DS18B20+?

The DS18B20+ features a user-programmable temperature resolution of 9, 10, 11, or 12 bits, corresponding to temperature increments of 0.5°C, 0.25°C, 0.125°C, and 0.0625°C, respectively. Higher resolution increases conversion time, with maximum conversion times of 93.75ms at 9-bit resolution, 187.5ms at 10-bit, 375ms at 11-bit, and 750ms at 12-bit.

How does the 1-Wire protocol handle multi-drop network architecture and individual sensor identification?

The DS18B20+ uses Maxim's proprietary 1-Wire open-drain serial interface, requiring only a single data line (DQ) and ground for communication. Each device contains a factory-programmed, unique 64-bit ROM code consisting of an 8-bit family code (28h), a 48-bit serial number, and an 8-bit CRC. This unique identifier allows multiple DS18B20+ devices to share the same 1-Wire bus while being individually addressed by the bus master. The bus requires an external pullup resistor of approximately 5kΩ.
