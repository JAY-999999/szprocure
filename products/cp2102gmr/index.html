Frequently Asked Questions

What internal controllers, clocking components, and USB functions are integrated into the CP2102?

The Silicon Labs CP2102 is a highly integrated USB 2.0 full-speed (12 Mbps) USB-to-UART bridge controller. It integrates a USB function controller, USB transceiver, calibrated internal 48 MHz oscillator, on-chip 3.3 V voltage regulator, power-on reset circuit, and an internal 1024-byte EEPROM. The device requires no external crystal or USB resistors for USB communication, helping minimize external components and PCB space.

What operating voltages, power modes, and current consumption does the CP2102 support?

The CP2102 supports both bus-powered and self-powered operation. In bus-powered mode, the REGIN pin accepts 4.0 V to 5.25 V from the USB bus, while self-powered designs supply 3.0 V to 3.6 V directly to the VDD pin. The on-chip voltage regulator provides a nominal 3.3 V output and can supply up to 100 mA to external circuitry. Typical operating current is 20 mA, while typical suspend current is 80 µA (bus-powered with the regulator enabled).

What UART baud rates, data formats, and flow-control options are supported?

The CP2102 UART supports programmable baud rates from 300 bps up to 921600 bps. It includes a 576-byte receive buffer and a 640-byte transmit buffer, and supports 5-, 6-, 7-, or 8-bit data, 1, 1.5, or 2 stop bits, and odd, even, mark, space, or no parity. Hardware flow control (RTS/CTS and DSR/DTR), modem interface signals (DCD and RI), and software XON/XOFF handshaking are also supported.
