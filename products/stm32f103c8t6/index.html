Product Introduction

The STM32F103C8T6 is a member of ST's STM32F103xx medium-density performance line: a 32-bit microcontroller built around an Arm Cortex-M3 core running at up to 72 MHz, with 64 KB of embedded Flash and 20 KB of SRAM. It integrates two 12-bit ADCs, three general-purpose 16-bit timers plus one advanced-control PWM timer, and communication interfaces including two SPI, two I2C, three USARTs, one USB and one CAN, operating from a 2.0-3.6 V supply in a 48-pin LQFP package.

Frequently Asked Questions

What package does STM32F103C8T6 use

STM32F103C8T6 is supplied in a 48-pin LQFP-48 (Low-profile Quad Flat Package) surface-mount package.

What CPU core and maximum speed does the STM32F103C8T6 use?

An Arm Cortex-M3 32-bit RISC core running at up to 72 MHz (1.25 DMIPS/MHz).

How much Flash and SRAM does the C8T6 have?

64 KB of embedded Flash and 20 KB of SRAM (the 'C8' denotes the 64 KB Flash medium-density variant).
